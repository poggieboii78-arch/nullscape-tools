# Solo Extreme Meta — Draft

> **UNPUBLISHED DRAFT — Solo Extreme only**
>
> This is a research draft, not a statement of universal meta. Generic upgrade tier lists are deliberately not used as the primary authority here. The route is based on recent solo-run discussion, reported shop totals, and cross-checking against the existing upgrade data.

## Core idea

Solo Extreme has a very strong early economy. The goal is not to blindly buy the highest-ranked individual upgrade: it is to turn early income into enough mobility and safety to keep collecting gifts, then snowball into the late game.

## Upgrade priority

### S+ — Core solo economy / run enablers

- **Paycheck** — extremely high early value because it starts paying back immediately and has the whole run to compound.
- **Double Jump** — one of the most important survival/mobility purchases; especially valuable once the economy can afford it early.
- **Business License** — very strong when purchased early enough to affect several later shops; second/third stack is a greedier choice in solo because mobility can be worth more for consistency.

### S — High priority

- **Grace Wings** — major solo mobility and gap insurance; commonly part of the level-8 power spike.
- **Pocket Bell** — unusually strong in solo once affordable, especially for safer altar/purge greed.
- **Swiftness Ring** — stackable mobility that also improves gift collection; buy according to available economy rather than forcing it over survival upgrades.
- **Helmet** — reliable late-game defensive value; generally becomes more important after the early economy is established.

### A — Strong / context dependent

- **Ice Skates** — excellent when the run/map benefits from them; not worth delaying core economy or essential mobility for blindly.
- **Fanny Pack** — expensive but potentially excellent once the economy is snowballing and the extra capacity can be filled.
- **Defuse Kit** — strong when traps/Tripmines are a meaningful threat.
- **Radar / Radar Modules** — luxury-to-strong depending on the run; better once core mobility/economy is established.
- **Grapple Points / Larger Grapple Points** — class/map dependent; much more valuable when the movement route actually benefits from them.
- **Tria Orbs** — strong luxury purchase on an unusually rich run.

### B — Useful but not automatic

- **Adrenaline** — excellent upgrade in general, but **NOT the default level-3 Solo Extreme purchase**. Its individual tier-list ranking should not be confused with optimal shop sequencing.
- **Medal** — can become extremely valuable when the run is deliberately built around its curse/economy interaction; less attractive if taking harder curses compromises consistency.
- **More Altars / Radar Module: Altars** — increasingly valuable as the run's economy grows and altar strategies become available.
- **Panic Necklace**
- **Shield**
- **Ninja Belt**
- **Gift Magnet**
- **Matrix Tetrahedron**

### C — Situational / luxury

- **Orb**
- **Miniature Hourglass**
- **Last Robloxian Standing**
- **Sport Shoes**
- **Shark Tail**
- **Subspacial Barrier**
- **Drowned Aegis**

## Suggested shopping route

### Level 3

**Default:** Paycheck.

If the run has unusually good gift income, the important question becomes whether the extra economy can support an early mobility purchase without delaying the income engine.

A commonly reported solo route reaches roughly 190 Golden Gifts around this point and can fit **Paycheck + Adrenaline + Business License**; however, this should be treated as a rich-run route rather than evidence that Adrenaline is universally the correct first purchase.

### Level 5

Typical priority pool:

1. **Double Jump**
2. **Paycheck / additional Paycheck**
3. **Business License**
4. **Medal**, when pursuing the medal-curses strategy
5. **Swiftness Ring**
6. **Better Jump Pads / Defuse Kit** when the economy supports them

The exact order depends heavily on gift RNG and whether the run is being played for consistency or maximum greed.

### Level 8

This is the major solo power spike. A strong economy can support a package around:

- **Grace Wings**
- **Pocket Bell**
- **Ice Skates**
- **Swiftness Ring stack**
- additional **Paycheck / Business License**
- **Fanny Pack** on especially rich runs
- luxury movement/utility such as Grapple Points or Tria Orbs

### Level 10+

Convert the economy into consistency:

- **Helmet**
- Pocket Bell if not already owned
- altar/radar infrastructure
- remaining mobility gaps
- defensive upgrades
- then greed/luxury purchases when the run is already stable

## Greed strategies

### Medal curse route

Medal can be used as part of a deliberate curse-pool strategy. This can lead into **Nothing?** and eventually **LAP 2**, producing a much larger economy.

This is **not automatically the best solo route**. The tradeoff is that deliberately selecting harder enemies/curses can reduce the consistency of a run whose entire economy depends on surviving long enough to spend it.

### Fragile Gifts

Potentially very strong because of the increased Golden Gift yield, but the faster collapse / gift fragility can make the theoretical income less useful if collection becomes unreliable.

### Nothing?

High-ceiling greed option. The shop discount can compound enormously over a long run, but the added pressure makes it a poor default for consistency.

### Purgatory / altar greed

Once the economy and movement are established, altar manipulation becomes one of the biggest sources of additional income. **Pocket Bell** is particularly attractive here in solo because it makes certain altar interactions safer.

## Class notes

For Solo Extreme, class ranking should be judged primarily by consistency and ability to collect gifts while escaping danger. Do not copy a multiplayer class ranking blindly.

The draft deliberately leaves class ordering less absolute than the upgrade ordering because the optimal class depends strongly on player skill and movement preference.

## Important correction

**Adrenaline is not listed as the Solo Extreme L3 meta buy.** It is a strong general upgrade, but recent solo-run reports show a much more economy-first route, with Paycheck as the default early purchase and mobility/economy being sequenced around the actual Golden Gift total.

## Evidence / research notes

- Recent solo discussion reports that solo economy is extremely strong and that players can reach combinations such as Pocket Bell, Grace Wings, and multiple Swiftness Rings around level 8.
- A detailed solo calculation posted in that discussion reports approximately 190 Golden Gifts around level 3 and a route involving Paycheck, Adrenaline, Business License, then Double Jump, Swiftness Ring, Medal, and additional Paycheck by level 5. This supports the economy-first approach while showing that Adrenaline can fit when the run is rich.
- The same discussion reports roughly 1200 Golden Gifts by level 8 in a strong run and a package involving Wings, Pocket Bell, Ice Skates, Swiftness Ring, Defuse Kit, Paycheck, and then luxury movement/utility.
- Generic public tier lists rank Adrenaline highly, but those lists are not solo-sequencing guides and should not override actual solo-run economy reports.
